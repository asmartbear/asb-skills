## What this is about

Ask a customer "Blogs get hacked all the time and it's devastating,
right? Would you like extra security?" and everyone says yes. They
would look foolish otherwise. You led the witness to the answer you
wanted. Now you will never know the answer you needed. Most customer
interviews fail here. The problem is not who you talk to. The problem
is questions that hint at the wanted answer and collect polite
agreement instead of real validation.

This is the third step of the interview method. The method runs goals
→ hypotheses → questions. Every question links to a hypothesis. This
makes writing good questions easier, not guesswork. Each question is a
small experiment. The book sets four criteria for each question. The
question must be able to confirm or deny the hypothesis. The question
must not hint at any particular answer. The question must draw out
specific facts, not feelings. The question must invite information you
did not know to ask for. The skill drafts candidate questions. It
tests every version, including yours, with a polite-stranger test. It
phrases each question in your customers' own words. It will not
finalize a question that breaks the criteria. A leading question does
not just weaken an interview. It creates false evidence. The source
allows one exception: near the end of an interview, you may state a
specific price. The customer's gut reaction to a real number is itself
useful data.

Give the skill your whole HYPOTHESES.md file and it works through the
list. It groups related hypotheses so the set fits a real
conversation. It orders the result like an interview plan. It keeps
QUESTIONS.md updated from the first finished question, so a long
session, or one you resume later, never loses work. Or give it a
single hypothesis. The skill then shapes that one question with you
and gives it back in chat. No files, no extra steps.

## Example invocation

You can invoke the skill like this:

```text
/asb-interview-questions Here's my HYPOTHESES.md from the hypotheses
exercise — turn these into the questions I'll actually ask.
```

The session works through your hypotheses one at a time. It drafts and tests each question until the question is truly open-ended. Then it writes QUESTIONS.md as a ready-to-use interview plan.

Or bring one hypothesis with no file at all:

```text
/asb-interview-questions My hypothesis: gym owners spend at least 2
hours a week chasing failed membership payments. How do I ask about
this without planting the answer?
```

In single-question mode, the skill shapes one phrasing with you and hands back the final question in chat.

One practical note: say where you are working. Name a directory, or
point at your existing files, when you invoke the skill. It then keeps
the method's files together there. If you do not say where, it asks
before it creates anything.

- [**When you have nothing: How to find potential customers to interview**](https://longform.asmartbear.com/find-customers-to-interview/) — where to find people to ask, once the questions are ready.
