## What this is about

In high-school debate, the last speaker gets three minutes. The
opponent gets seven. You cannot answer every point. So the winning
tactic, "the Voter," changes the whole contest. It comes down to one
or two key points. You choose the points where you are strongest. Win
those points, and you win the debate. You can lose every other point
and still win. Business works the same way. Steve Jobs was obsessed
with industrial design. This obsession made the iPhone dominant.
iTunes was one of the worst pieces of software ever shipped. But the
iPhone still won. The voter did not fix the weakness. It overpowered
the weakness.

Most founders cannot name their voter. Some offer five strengths
("quality, speed, our people…"). Every competitor claims the same
strengths. Others name a real strength that is still common. Many
people know your platform. Many founders like to charge premium
prices. These traits shape a strategy. But they do not make you
special. A voter is different. It is an extreme trait, earned through
years of obsession. It is rare — peers would name you as the one who
has it. It is decisive — it must overpower real trade-offs that
customers accept. And it is powerful — it forces decisions in your
product, price, and market. At its strongest, your ideal customer
reads your weaknesses as proof of the voter. On a handmade
marketplace, the imperfection is the point.

The skill always gives a third verdict, between the voter and the
common trait. This is the *special strength*. It is a near miss. It
is genuinely in the top 1%, but it fails one decisive test. For
example, it may be fairly rare, but not the rarest in the world. A
special strength is not a voter. Do not inflate it into one. But do
not file it away and forget it either. It is a secondary pillar. The
strategy should build on it deliberately. It makes the plan better,
even though it is not the main bet.

This skill tests your strengths, experiences, and obsessions against
that gauntlet. It checks one candidate at a time. It does not stop at
a comfortable list. It limits the answer to two voters. It presses
every soft claim hard, until the answer is specific and solid. When
nothing survives the test, the skill says so plainly. Nothing is
special and extreme yet. Then it gives two options. Invest your
deepest genuine obsession until it becomes extreme. Or change to a
game you are already built to win.

## Example invocation

You can invoke the skill like this:

```text
/asb-voters Here's my WHO-ME.md and our STRENGTHS-WEAKNESSES.md. I
think our voter is "craftsmanship" but honestly every agency says
that. What actually makes us the ones to bet on?
```

The skill gathers every candidate from your files and stories. It runs
each candidate through the five-test gauntlet. You end with
`VOTERS.md`. This file lists at most two voters, with their evidence
and consequences. It also includes the strategy audit that starts
from them.

One practical note: say where you are working. Name a directory, or
point to your existing files, when you invoke the skill. The skill
then keeps the method's files together in that place. If you do not
say where, the skill asks before it creates anything.

## From the source

Two sources form the foundation of this skill:

- As in Chapter 11 of *Hidden Multipliers*: *To Thine Own Self Be True* (section "Play Your Game") — [hiddenmultipliers.com](https://hiddenmultipliers.com). This section covers the Voter tactic itself, the Jobs and Etsy cases, why the cap is one or two, and why weaknesses can read as strengths when they serve a voter.
- [**Worse, but unique**](https://longform.asmartbear.com/worse-but-unique/) — the King's Gambit argument. A "refuted" strategy can win at your level because nobody prepares for it. Extremity, not mere difference, is what maximizes the few things that make you special.

Supporting articles each supply a piece of the mechanism:

- [**The three kinds of leverage that anchor effective strategies**](https://longform.asmartbear.com/leverage/) — why the story of every winner comes down to one or two things, never the 47 details. This article also gives the Teller line this skill uses as its extremity bar: magic is spending more time on something than anyone would reasonably expect.
- [**Pivot Points**](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pivot-points/) — the raw material. These are the plain facts of who you are, without judgment. Voters come from these facts. Everything else pivots around them.
