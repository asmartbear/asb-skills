---

This policy is for the Google OAuth app "ClaudeMCP". Jason Cohen made this app
for his own use. No other person uses it.

## What data the app reads

The app reads and changes data in Jason's own Google account: Gmail, Docs,
Drive, Sheets, Slides, Calendar and YouTube. It does this only when Jason runs
his own tools.

## Where the data goes

The data stays on Jason's own computer. The app does not sell the data. The
app does not give the data to other people. The app does not use the data for
advertisements.
